# 📊 Global Retail Sales Performance & Executive Insights Dashboard

An end-to-end Data Analytics project leveraging advanced Microsoft Excel to clean, transform, and model multi-country supermarket transactional datasets exceeding **₹20.62 Crores (INR)** in gross revenue.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Advanced Excel Skills Applied
* **Data Cleaning & Standardization:** Utilized `TRIM()`, uppercase formatting, duplication checks, and structural data alignment. Standardized diverse international currencies into a single uniform reporting currency (INR).
* **Data Transformation:** Designed an organized schema across separate layered sheets to separate raw logs from reporting zones.
* **Analytical Modeling:** Engineered 5 multi-dimensional Pivot Tables to track Monthly Performance, Category Volume, Territorial Contribution, Net Profit Margins, and Customer Purchasing Behavior.
* **Dashboard Architecture (UI/UX):** Developed high-contrast executive KPI Cards, disabled gridlines for an application-like interface, and embedded advanced multi-axis visuals.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Deployed interactive Slicers (`City`, `Customer Type`, `Payment Method`) completely synchronized via full **Report Connections** to prevent visualization breaks.

---

## 📈 Executive Summary & Deep Business Insights

### 1. The Core Revenue Engines (Electronics & Clothing)
* **Insight:** Electronics alone drove a staggering **₹18.99 Crores**, followed by Clothing. Together, they command **over 85%+** of total sales volume, peaking massively during the month of **April**.
* **Action Plan:** Implement predictive inventory stocking 3 weeks prior to April to eliminate stockouts. Optimize supply chain logistics for Electronics to maintain this highly lucrative velocity.

### 2. Identifying the Hidden Underperformer (Stationery Strategy)
* **Insight:** Stationery product sales are underperforming across all quarters, flatlining between **₹10,000 to **₹20,000** monthly (contributing <2% to total revenue).
* **Action Plan (Cross-Selling & Product Bundling):** Launch an executive high-margin product bundle. Pair premium tech stationery (diaries/pens) as an add-on item with high-ticket Electronics (Laptops/Tablets) at a 15% combined discount at the point of sale.

### 3. Financial Health Metrics
* **Insight:** Out of **₹20,62,09,979.90** total revenue, the business captured a Net Profit of **₹4,38,44,719.90**, establishing a highly robust and lucrative **21.2% Net Profit Margin**.

## 📂 Repository Architecture
* `01_Raw_Data` : Raw transactional logs with multi-currency and formatting inconsistencies.
* `02_Cleaned_Data` : 100% sanitized, standardized database ready for analytical ingestion.
* `03_Pivot_Summary` : Summary schemas answering targeted C-suite business questions.
* `04_Sales_Dashboard` : Fully dynamic, interactive retail dashboard with optimized KPI positioning.
